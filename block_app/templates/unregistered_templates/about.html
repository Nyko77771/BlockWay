About BlockWay

BlockWay is a smart DNS security application designed to help users manage unwanted websites and improve online protection.

By combining Pi-hole DNS filtering with machine learning, BlockWay analyses unknown domains and identifies potential threats before they reach your network.

The application provides users with an easy-to-use dashboard where they can monitor blocked domains, review threats, and manage their network security.
